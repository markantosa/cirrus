TECH Zoomers Challenge
The TECH Zoomers Challenge is the ultimate stage for TECH Zoomer Bootcamp 
participants to turn bold ideas into real impact. This exciting competition invites university 
students to tackle real semiconductor industry challenges with creative thinking, 
teamwork, and sharp presentation skills.
Participants will form teams to solve actual problems provided by top semiconductor 
companies. With guidance from industry mentors, they’ll brainstorm and pitch their 
solutions to a panel of expert judges.
It’s more than just a challenge; it’s a launchpad for future innovators. Students will gain 
hands-on experience, sharpen critical skills, and connect with industry leaders who are 
shaping the world of technology.
Winning teams will walk away with prizes, recognition, and career-boosting exposure. 
This is where the next generation of tech changemakers begins!

How To Make A Poster In Canva

How To Create Canva 
Presentations 2026 (Step-By-Step)
Here's a step-by-step guide to create an A1 poster:
1. Sign in or Sign up:
• Log in to your Canva account or create a free account if you don't 
have one. 
2. Start a New Design:
• Click on "Create a design" and choose "Poster" or "Custom size". 
3. Choose Custom Size:
• If you choose "Custom size", enter the A1 dimensions (841mm x 
594mm) in the width and height fields. 
4. Choose a Template (Optional):
• If you prefer, you can select a pre-designed A1 poster template from 
Canva's library. 
5. Customize Your Design:
• Images: Upload your own images or use Canva's free and premium 
image library. 
• Text: Add text, change fonts, and adjust colors to match your style. 
• Elements: Explore Canva's design elements like shapes, icons, and 
stickers to add visual flair. 
6. Save and Download:
• Once you're happy with your design, download it as a PDF Print
